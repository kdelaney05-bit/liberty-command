About the app

TruReview is the field app for the sales representatives of Liberty Fencing, Liberty Roofing, Pro-Tech Roofing and Oasis Landscapes. It shows each representative their appointment book, follow-ups and team scoreboard. Accounts are created by the company — there is no public sign-up.

Trouble signing in, or anything else

Email kdelaney05@gmail.com and you will get a reply within one business day. That address reaches the person who runs the system, not a queue.

If you are on the team and locked out, you can also just tell your office — access problems are fixed the same day.
