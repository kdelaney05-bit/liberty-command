TRUREVIEW PRIVACY POLICY

THE FIELD APP FOR OUR SALES TEAM

Who this app is for

TruReview is a work tool used by the sales representatives of Liberty Fencing, Liberty Roofing, Pro-Tech Roofing and Oasis Landscapes. There is no public sign-up: every account is created by the company for a member of its team. This policy says plainly what the app collects, why, and what never happens with it.

What the app collects, and why

  • Your account. Name, work email and phone number, set up by the company so the app knows who you are.
  • Location. Precise location, including in the background, used for one thing: confirming that a representative has arrived at a scheduled customer appointment. It is compared against that day's appointment addresses and nothing else.
  • Camera and photos. Photos you take or attach are job documentation — evidence of work, estimates and invoices — stored in the company's database.
  • Customer records. Customer names, addresses and phone numbers come from the company's scheduling system so representatives can serve the appointments assigned to them.
  • Work activity. Appointments, follow-ups, sales results, and business calls and texts made through company office numbers are recorded for coaching and team scoreboards.
  • A push token. A device identifier that lets the app deliver team notifications to your phone.
  • App usage and speed. Time spent in the app and how fast it runs on your phone — screen-time heartbeats and performance measurements, visible to company management only, kept briefly, and used for one thing: making sure a slow phone or a struggling rollout gets fixed before it costs anyone their day.

What never happens

  • No data is sold, ever, to anyone.
  • No advertising and no ad trackers of any kind.
  • No sharing with third parties, except the service providers that run the system itself: Supabase (database hosting) and Apple / Expo (push-notification delivery). They process data to operate the app and for no other purpose.

How access is controlled

Data lives in a hosted database protected by row-level security: the database itself decides what each signed-in person may see, based on their role. A representative sees their own book of business; owners see the company view. Nobody sees more than their role allows.

Retention and deletion

Business records are kept as long as the company needs them for its operations. If you want your personal data reviewed, corrected or deleted, email the address below and it will be handled directly.
