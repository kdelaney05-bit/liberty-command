One thing about the number
Texts from the Range go out on the company line, not your cell. A customer who's used to your cell sees a new number once. Your first text to them says:
"This is my work number. Text me here anytime, I monitor it. You can still call me on either one."
After that, text from the Range and only the Range. If half the conversation is on your cell, the file only sees half.
