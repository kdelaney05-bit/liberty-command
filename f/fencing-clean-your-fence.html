How to Clean Your Fence

A fence is one of the few upgrades that looks better the longer you take care of it — and taking care of it is easier than most people think. Here's the right way to clean each kind.

PVC & vinyl

A garden hose, a soft brush and a little dish soap handle almost everything. For scuffs and stubborn marks, a melamine sponge (magic eraser) works wonders. Skip harsh solvents — they can dull the finish — and keep pressure washers on a wide, gentle setting from a few feet back.

Aluminum

The easiest of all: rinse it a couple of times a year, mild soapy water where grime builds near sprinklers, and it keeps its finish for decades.

Wood

Gentle wash with soapy water and a brush. Go easy with pressure — blasting up close furs the grain. When water stops beading on the surface, that's the fence telling you it's time to re-seal or re-stain.

💡 Florida tip: in our humidity, the shady side of the fence grows algae and mildew first. Catch it early with a quick seasonal rinse and it never gets a foothold.
