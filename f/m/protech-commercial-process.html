How We Run a Commercial Roof

Two minutes, start to closeout.

This is what happens after you award us roofing. Written for the builder, not the owner, because you're the one who lives with the schedule.

1

Scope review, line by line

Before mobilization we sit with your PM and walk the roof scope against the drawings and our proposal: deck, insulation, tapered, flashing details, tie-ins, warranty term. Gaps get found here, not in a change order.

2

Submittals and schedule, in one packet

Product data, shop drawings where required, the safety program, our COI, and a crew schedule against your milestones. One packet, one contact, the same week we're awarded.

3

Site planning before the first truck

Staging, crane or hoist location, tenant and traffic hours, dry-in sequencing with your framers or trades. On an occupied building we plan around the people in it.

4

A named superintendent and a dedicated crew

The same crew from start to finish, run by a superintendent you can call. No rotating labor. A daily photo log you can forward to the owner without editing a word.

5

Weather and tie-ins handled like grown-ups

Nothing opened that can't be dried in the same day. Tie-ins documented. If the forecast moves, you hear it from us before you see it on the roof.

6

Manufacturer inspection and closeout

The manufacturer inspects before we call it done. Warranty, as-builts and the photo log go to you in one closeout package, so the owner has it the day the building turns over.
