Commercial Warranties — what the owner actually gets

Three warranties, one closeout.

Every commercial roof we install leaves with the manufacturer's warranty, our workmanship warranty, and the paperwork that makes both real. Here is what each one covers, in plain words, so you can answer the owner without calling us.

Manufacturer NDL — "no dollar limit"

The one specs ask for. The manufacturer inspects the finished roof, then warrants the whole system — membrane, insulation, flashings — for 10, 15, 20, 25 or 30 years depending on system and term, with no cap on the repair cost. Only certified installers can offer it. We are one.

Manufacturer material warranty

Covers the membrane itself against manufacturing defects. Standard on every system, no inspection required. It's the floor, not the ceiling — if a spec says "manufacturer warranty" with no term, ask whether they mean this or the NDL. We'll help you read it.

Pro-Tech workmanship warranty

Our own, on our labor: flashings, seams, penetrations, tie-ins. Term stated on the proposal. If it leaks because of how it was installed, that's ours, and we come back.

What a GC should check before buyout

  • The warranty term in the spec, and whether it requires an NDL (most 20-year-and-up terms do).
  • That the installer is certified for the specified manufacturer — the NDL is void otherwise.
  • Whether the insulation and cover board are the manufacturer's approved components — a mixed system can break the warranty.
  • That the inspection is in the schedule before turnover, not after.
