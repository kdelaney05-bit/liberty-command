Commercial Roof Systems — which one, and why

TPO (single-ply, heat-welded)

Best for: new low-slope construction, big-box retail, warehouses, most tenant improvements

White reflective membrane, welded seams, mechanically fastened or fully adhered over insulation. The workhorse of Florida commercial roofing: fast to install, energy-code friendly, and the easiest system to price alternates on (thickness, attachment, insulation R-value).

Ask us: 60 vs 80 mil, adhered vs fastened, and the tapered insulation package — those three lines are where a TPO number moves.

PVC (single-ply, heat-welded)

Best for: restaurants, kitchens, anything with grease or chemical exposure, owners who want the longer warranty

Same installation family as TPO with a membrane that shrugs off grease, oils and standing water. Costs more per square; earns it back on restaurant roofs and where a 25- or 30-year NDL warranty is specified.

Ask us: whether the spec's warranty term forces PVC — if it does, we'll say so before you carry TPO by mistake.

Modified bitumen (2-ply, torch or self-adhered)

Best for: re-roofs over existing built-up roofs, small flat roofs, high-traffic roofs with equipment

Two asphalt plies with a granulated cap sheet. Tough underfoot, forgiving of rooftop traffic and mechanical work, and the natural choice when you're recovering an old built-up roof rather than tearing down to the deck.

Ask us: torch vs self-adhered — on an occupied building, self-adhered keeps the open flame off the roof.

Standing seam metal

Best for: sloped roofs, storefront and canopy work, anything the owner wants to look at for 40 years

Concealed-fastener panels, roll-formed to length. The premium option: highest wind ratings, longest life, the cleanest look. Priced by panel profile, gauge and finish, so the alternates are real.

Ask us: profile and gauge — a 24-gauge Kynar panel and a 26-gauge SMP panel are two different numbers and two different roofs.

Restoration coatings (silicone / acrylic)

Best for: an owner who needs another 10–15 years without a tear-off, budget-driven re-roofs, phasing

A fluid-applied membrane over a sound existing roof. No tear-off, no dumpsters, no disruption below. It is not a new roof and we won't call it one, but on the right substrate it is the cheapest decade of dry building an owner can buy.

Ask us: for a core cut and moisture survey first — a coating over a wet roof is money thrown away, and we'll tell you if that's what we find.
