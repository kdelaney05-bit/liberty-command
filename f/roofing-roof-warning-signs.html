Five Signs Your Roof Wants Attention

Roofs rarely fail overnight — they send warnings first. Here are the five worth acting on, all visible without a ladder.

1 · Granules in the gutters

Those coarse sand-like grains are your shingles' sun armor wearing away. A little is normal; a lot, or bald-looking patches, means the shingles are aging out.

2 · Curling or cracked shingles

Edges lifting, corners curling, cracks across the surface — shingles that have lost their flexibility can't seal out wind-driven rain.

3 · Dark streaks

Common in Florida — usually algae. Mostly cosmetic, but streaks that stay wet or trace one path can mark where water is lingering.

4 · Signs inside

Ceiling stains, a musty attic, or daylight showing through the roof boards — any of these means water is already finding a way in.

5 · Shingles in the yard

Finding pieces after a windy day means the roof is losing its grip. That's the roof asking for an inspection, soon.

💡 Most roofs earn a professional look at 15–20 years old — earlier if any of the five above show up.
