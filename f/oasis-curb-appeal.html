Five Quick Wins for Curb Appeal

You don't need a full redesign to change how your home reads from the street. These five moves do the most with the least.

1 · Crisp bed edges

A clean cut line between grass and bed is the cheapest transformation in landscaping — the whole yard instantly reads "kept."

2 · Fresh mulch

New mulch is the landscaping equivalent of a fresh coat of paint: instant contrast that makes every plant in the bed pop.

3 · Color at the entry

One concentrated pop of flowers at the front door or mailbox beats the same plants scattered thin across the yard. Put the color where eyes already go.

4 · Light one tree

A single uplight on a palm or oak changes the whole house after dark — it's the highest-drama dollar in the yard.

5 · Clear the sightlines

Trim anything that blocks the view of the front door. Homes read welcoming when the entry is visible from the street.
